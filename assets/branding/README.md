# Provisional Solar Care branding

These assets share a green, white, and yellow solar mark. Replace them with business-approved artwork before publication. Paths are defined in `src/constants/metadata.json`.

- `icon.png` is the full-bleed launcher and fallback icon.
- `adaptive-background.png` is the Android adaptive background.
- `adaptive-foreground.png` is the transparent Android adaptive foreground.
- `adaptive-monochrome.png` is the alpha-based layer for themed Android icons.
- `splash-mark.png` is the transparent mark used by the native and in-app splash screens.
